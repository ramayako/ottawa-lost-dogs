Let an AI watch for your dog

After you post a lost dog, press Get AI watch instructions. Paste the text into Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant that can browse the web and ask it to check every few hours. It reads this board's open data and tells you when a matching dog is posted.

While this page is open, it also checks every two minutes and shows an alert at the top.
